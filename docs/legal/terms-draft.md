Flowgit is open-source software (Apache-2.0). These terms cover only Flowgit Cloud (the signed-in service at flowgit.dev), not the Flowgit CLI, the local board or the signed-out web app that you run yourself. The service is provided by [operator name and place].

## 2. 测试版 / Beta

云端现在是凭邀请码的免费测试版。功能会变，可能中断，也可能丢数据；请在自己的电脑或 Git 仓库里保留重要流程的副本。我们可以调整或结束测试版，结束前至少提前 [30] 天通知，让你导出数据。

The cloud is a free, invite-only beta. Features change, the service may be interrupted and data may be lost; keep copies of important flows on your computer or in a Git repository. We may change or end the beta, with at least [30] days' notice so you can export your data.

你用 GitHub 或 Google 登录，要保管好这些账号。一个人一个账号，不要转让邀请码去绕过测试版的限制。你要年满 [16] 岁，或者在你所在地可以自己同意这类服务的年龄。

You sign in with GitHub or Google and are responsible for those accounts. One person, one account; don't pass invite codes around to get past the beta's limits. You must be at least [16], or old enough to agree to services like this where you live.

## 4. 你的内容 / Your content

你放进云端的流程、提示词、文件和产物还是你的。你允许我们为了提供服务而存储、复制、处理和展示它们（例如保存版本、给你邀请的人看），仅此而已。我们不拿你的内容训练模型，也不卖给别人。工作区的拥有者决定谁能看、谁能改。

The flows, prompts, files and outputs you put in the cloud stay yours. You allow us to store, copy, process and show them only to run the service (for example, keeping versions and showing them to people you invite). We don't train models on your content or sell it. A workspace's owners decide who can view and edit it.

## 5. 可以和不可以 / Acceptable use

不要用云端做违法的事、侵犯别人的权利、传播恶意软件、攻击或探测我们和别人的系统、绕过配额和权限，或者存放你无权分享的个人信息。我们可以删掉违规内容，暂停或关闭违规账号。

Don't use the cloud to break the law, infringe others' rights, spread malware, attack or probe our systems or anyone else's, get around quotas and permissions, or store personal data you have no right to share. We may remove content that breaks these rules and suspend or close accounts that do.

## 6. 配额 / Quotas

测试版有上限（现在是每个工作区 2 GB、200 个流程，单个文件 50 MB，以及请求频率）。上限可能调整，会写在 `docs/cloud.md`。

The beta has limits (currently 2 GB and 200 flows per workspace, 50 MB per file, plus request rates). They may change and are listed in `docs/cloud.md`.

Flows can call the models and services you choose (for example DeepSeek or Anthropic). Those services have their own terms and prices, and when you use your own key, your agreement with them applies. Model output can be wrong; check it before you rely on it.
