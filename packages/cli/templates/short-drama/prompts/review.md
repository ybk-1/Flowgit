内容导向逐项看（依据广电总局对微短剧的管理提示）/ Content guidance, item by item (NRTA notices on micro-dramas):

- 有没有渲染奢华、拜金、权势压人（“霸总”类提示）。
- 有没有低俗、色情、血腥暴力的画面或台词被当作看点。
- 人设和剧情有没有极端“反智”“雷人”“离谱”的地方。
- 对老人、孩子、特定职业或地区有没有贬低、刻板印象。
- Glamorized wealth, money worship or bullying by the powerful.
- Vulgar, sexual or gory images or lines used as the draw.
- Absurd, "anti-intellectual" characters or plots.
- Disparaging or stereotyping the elderly, children, jobs or regions.

创作质量逐项看 / Craft, item by item:

- 每集前 3 秒有没有冲突；第 1 集有没有讲清主角、目标和阻碍。
- 每集有没有压抑和爆发，爆发有没有铺垫，反转是否合理。
- 结尾悬念是否让人想看下一集；付费卡点那一集是否在情绪最高点停下。
- 人物动机是否说得通，台词是否像真人说话、够不够短。
- Conflict in the first 3 seconds; episode 1 establishes the lead, the goal and the obstacle.
- Suppression and payoff in each episode, payoffs set up, twists plausible.
- Cliffhangers that pull to the next episode; the paywall episode stops at the emotional peak.
- Motivations that make sense; short, natural dialogue.
