# 截图 / Screenshots

幻灯片要放的截图放在这里，文件名是幻灯片编号（见 `teleprompter.md` 里的【s2-1】或 `deck/index.html` 上的提示）：`s2-1.png`、`s2-1.jpg`、`s2-1.jpeg` 或 `s2-1.webp`。

放好以后，`deck/index.html` 刷新就能看到；生成视频时，没有录屏的段会把截图放在标题下面。

截图前把姓名、手机号、账号和余额这类个人信息遮掉。

Put the slides' screenshots here, named after the slide (the [s2-1] in `teleprompter.md`, or the note on the slide in `deck/index.html`): `s2-1.png`, `s2-1.jpg`, `s2-1.jpeg` or `s2-1.webp`.

Refresh `deck/index.html` to see them; when the video is rendered, a segment without a screen recording shows the screenshot under the headline.

Hide personal details such as names, phone numbers, account numbers and balances before you take a screenshot.
