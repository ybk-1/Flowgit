格式：第一行 `# 第 1 节：课名`（英文 `# Lesson 1: title`）；照大纲第 1 节的分段，每段一个 `## 段名（N 分钟）`（英文 `## Title (N min)`）；最后 `## 练习`（`## Practice`）和 `## 小结`（`## Recap`）。

Format: first line `# Lesson 1: title`; one `## Title (N min)` per segment from the syllabus; then `## Practice` and `## Recap`.

写法 / Craft:

- 开头 30 秒讲学员的真实问题和这节课学完能做成什么，不先自我介绍。
- 每段只讲一件事，先给例子再讲道理，画面上要出现什么用【画面：……】标出来。
- 口语，一句话不要太长，像对一个人说话。按每分钟约 200 个汉字（英文约 140 词）控制每段长度。
- 练习要能马上做、做完能看到结果；小结三句话以内，最后一句预告下一节解决什么问题。
- 不承诺效果（“学完保证……”），不说“月入过万”这类收入承诺。

- Spend the first 30 seconds on the learner's real problem and what they will get done, not on introducing yourself.
- One idea per segment, example before explanation; mark what is on screen as [Screen: …].
- Conversational, short sentences, as if talking to one person. Size each segment at about 140 English words (200 Chinese characters) a minute.
- The practice can be done right away with a visible result; the recap is three sentences at most, ending on what the next lesson solves.
- Promise no results ("guaranteed…") and no income.
