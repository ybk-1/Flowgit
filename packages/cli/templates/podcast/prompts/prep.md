访谈（interview）：写一封主持人发给嘉宾的录前说明，嘉宾直接能看。按顺序写这几部分，每部分一个 `## ` 小标题：

1. 这一期聊什么、为什么请你：两三句，用策划里的 `angle`。
2. 会聊的几个方向：只列方向，不要把完整的问题单发给嘉宾，这样回答更像聊天。
3. 时间和方式：录音大约多长；具体时间和链接由主持人另外发。不要编日期、时间或链接。
4. 录音环境：找一间安静、软装多的房间，关掉空调、风扇和冰箱一类的声音，别在厨房录；全程戴有线耳机，不要外放；手机和电脑关通知、开勿扰；远程录音时请嘉宾在自己那边也录一份音频；嘴离麦克风大约一拳（10 到 15 厘米），录音中保持这个距离。
5. 同意：请嘉宾回复确认同意录音，并同意剪辑后公开发布；说明节目会剪辑；请嘉宾提前说哪些事或哪些数字不能公开。
6. 资料核对：列出节目里会说到的、来自嘉宾的数字（从 `facts` 里挑），请嘉宾确认；说明这些数字播出时会讲明是嘉宾提供的。

独播（solo）：写一份主持人自己的录前清单，`## ` 小标题分成：录音环境（同上）、出声读一遍台本（标出换气和重音，读不顺的句子当场改短）、事实卡逐条核对出处、广告口播计时（有赞助时）。

For an interview: a note from the host to the guest that the guest can read as is. Write these parts in order, each under a `## ` heading:

1. What we'll talk about and why you: two or three sentences built on the plan's `angle`.
2. The directions we'll cover: directions only. Do not send the full question list, so the answers stay conversational.
3. When and how: roughly how long the recording takes; the host sends the exact time and link separately. Do not invent a date, a time or a link.
4. Your recording space: a quiet room with soft furnishings; turn off air conditioning, fans and the fridge, and stay out of the kitchen; wired headphones the whole time, never a speaker; notifications off and Do Not Disturb on; for a remote recording, record your own audio on your side too; keep your mouth about a fist (10 to 15 centimeters, 4 to 6 inches) from the microphone the whole time.
5. Consent: ask the guest to reply to confirm they agree to be recorded and to the edited episode being published; say the episode will be edited; ask them to say in advance anything, or any number, that must not be made public.
6. Fact check: list the numbers from the guest that the episode will state (pick them from `facts`) and ask the guest to confirm them; say that on air they will be credited to the guest.

For a solo show: the host's own checklist, with `## ` headings for the recording space (as above), reading the script aloud once (mark breaths and stresses, and shorten any sentence you stumble on), checking each fact card against its source, and timing the ad read (when there is a sponsor).
