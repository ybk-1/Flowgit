# Shen He on why her bookstore stopped discounting

Shen He, founder of Half Acre Books, explains how an independent bookstore keeps people coming back every month with an annual pass and a Thursday book club instead of discounts. If you run a small shop or plan to, it's a way to think about your own reason for people to return.

A store-wide half-price day in 2020 packed the shop, and almost none of those people came back. Since 2021 the shop has sold a 399-yuan annual pass; Shen He says it had 620 members at the end of 2025, with 71% renewing (from the shop's membership system, unaudited).
