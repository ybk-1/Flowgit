# Shen He on why her bookstore stopped discounting

- Show: Shop Notes, hosted by Lu Yao
- Guest: Shen He, founder of Half Acre Books
- Length: 30 minutes

## [0:00] Cold open (0.5 min)

(Play Shen He's own line)
Shen He: We tried a store-wide half-price day once. The shop was packed that day. And then almost none of those people ever came back.

## [0:30] Introduction (1 min)

Lu Yao: Welcome to Shop Notes. I'm Lu Yao.
My guest today is Shen He. She founded Half Acre Books.
We're talking about one thing: how an independent bookstore gets people to come back every month, without discounts.

## [1:30] How the shop started (5 min)

Transition: Let's start with how the shop began.

Questions:
- How did Half Acre Books get started?
  Follow-up: What kind of place did you want it to be at the start?
- What was hardest in those first two years?
  Follow-up: Why didn't the people from the half-price day come back?

Fact cards:
- Opened in Hangzhou in 2019, 120 square meters, about 8,000 titles on the shelves. (Notes: guest)
- A store-wide 50%-off clearance day in 2020 drew people who bought only discounted books and rarely came back. (Shen He, pre-interview call)

## [6:30] The annual pass (6 min)

Transition: After the discounts stopped, the shop found another way to keep people.

Questions:
- How did you decide the price and perks of the annual pass?
  Follow-up: Why one coffee a month instead of a bigger one-off gift?
- Do members think 399 yuan is too much?
  Follow-up: What do renewing members say about why they renew?

Fact cards:
- Since 2021: 399 yuan a year, 10% off books all year, one coffee a month, the book club free. (Shen He, pre-interview call, February 2026)
- 620 members at the end of 2025, 71% renewal. (Membership system export from Shen He, unaudited. Say on air that the figures are hers.)

## [13:30] The Thursday book club (7 min)

Transition: Back to the shop. Besides the pass, there's a reason to come back every week.

Questions:
- How did the Thursday book club start?
  Follow-up: What changed between five or six people and a full room?
- Why run it every week when the tickets don't make money on their own?
  Follow-up: What do book club regulars do in the shop afterwards?

Fact cards:
- Every Thursday night, 20 seats, free for members, 39 yuan with a drink for everyone else. (The shop's event page, January 2026)
- In 2025 membership fees were 12% of revenue and events 8%. (From Shen He, unaudited)

## [20:30] What she'd do less of (8 min)

Transition: To finish, what if she started over?

Questions:
- If you started over, what would you do less of?
  Follow-up: Why that one?
- What advice would you give a listener who wants to open a shop?
  Follow-up: Which moment taught you that?

Fact cards:
- Rent is 18,000 yuan a month. (Shen He, pre-interview call)

Note: if Shen He says half of Hangzhou's independent bookstores have closed, there's no source for it. Ask where she heard it, or say it's her impression.
