## 节目 / Show

- 开店手记（Shop Notes）：每期请一位开小店的人聊一件具体的事。主持人陆遥（Lu Yao）。/ Each episode, someone who runs a small shop talks about one specific thing. Host: Lu Yao.
- 听众：开小店或想开店的人。/ Listeners: people who run a small shop or plan to open one.

## 嘉宾 / Guest

- 沈禾（Shen He），半亩书店（Half Acre Books）创始人。书店 2019 年在杭州开业，120 平方米，在架约 8000 种书。/ Shen He founded Half Acre Books in Hangzhou in 2019: 120 square meters, about 8,000 titles on the shelves.
- 店里 4 个人，2 个全职。/ Four people work there, two of them full time.

## 事实和出处 / Facts and sources

- 2021 年开始做年卡会员，399 元一年：全年买书 9 折，每月一杯咖啡，读书会免费。出处：沈禾，2026 年 2 月采前沟通。/ An annual membership since 2021 at 399 yuan a year: 10% off books all year, one coffee a month, the book club free. Source: Shen He, pre-interview call, February 2026.
- 2025 年底会员 620 人，续费率 71%。出处：店里会员系统的导出，2025-12-31（沈禾提供，未经审计）。/ 620 members at the end of 2025, 71% renewal. Source: the shop's membership system export, 2025-12-31 (from Shen He, unaudited).
- 2025 年收入：书 50%，咖啡 30%，会员费 12%，活动 8%。出处：沈禾提供的 2025 年经营数据（未经审计）。/ 2025 revenue: books 50%, coffee 30%, membership fees 12%, events 8%. Source: Shen He's 2025 figures (unaudited).
- 每周四晚上办读书会，20 个座位，会员免费，非会员 39 元含一杯饮料。出处：书店的活动页，2026 年 1 月。/ A book club every Thursday night with 20 seats, free for members, 39 yuan with a drink for everyone else. Source: the shop's event page, January 2026.
- 房租每月 18000 元。出处：沈禾，采前沟通。/ Rent is 18,000 yuan a month. Source: Shen He, pre-interview call.
- 2020 年试过一次全场 5 折的“清仓日”。来的人大多只买打折书，之后很少再来；从那以后不再做全场打折。出处：沈禾，采前沟通。/ In 2020 they tried a store-wide 50%-off "clearance day". Most people who came bought only discounted books and rarely came back, so there have been no store-wide discounts since. Source: Shen He, pre-interview call.

## 待核实 / Unverified

- 沈禾说“杭州的独立书店这几年关了一半”，她自己也没数过，没有出处。节目里不要当成事实说。/ Shen He said "half of Hangzhou's independent bookstores have closed in recent years". She hasn't counted and there is no source. Do not say it as fact.
