# Lightcup Mini product weekly

- Meeting: Lightcup Mini product weekly
- Date: 2026-03-04
- Attendees: Lin Xia, Zhou Heng, Shen Yu

## Topics

### Trial and weight
- Point: 20 trial units went out and 14 replies came back. Support logged 6 questions about weight and 3 about crushing ice. The facts say about 420 grams, and commuters called it heavy. It cannot crush ice or nuts, and frozen fruit needs 5 minutes to thaw.
- Decision: The material stays Tritan and BPA free this week. This batch is sold as about 420 grams, and the page must not say it is already lighter. [7:30]
- Decision: Only the next version aims for 380 grams. The battery stays 2000 mAh, and this batch's mold does not change. [8:50]

### Colors
- Point: Cream and mist blue are both tooled. Mist blue led the trial by 4 votes.
- Decision: This batch keeps cream and mist blue, side by side on the page, and does not say most popular. [12:30]

### Launch
- Point: There is no basis for a lowest-price claim. Units sold, stock and review counts are not invented.
- Decision: Launch stays 2026-03-18 at 99 yuan, and the first 100 orders get a sleeve. No previous price and no units-sold number. [15:20]

### Support wording
- Point: The cup will not start if it is not seated. A full charge is about 3 hours and about 15 cups. Unmeasured numbers stay out.
- Decision: Support answers only facts already on record and otherwise says it will check and reply. The answers add no new promise. If the weight estimate is more than 20 grams off 380, the next meeting picks it up; the sketch is still due this week. [24:10]
