Write the job ad. Reply with Markdown only.

- Role: {{inputs.role}}
- Company: {{inputs.company}}
- Output language: {{inputs.language}} (zh = Chinese, en = English). Everything in that language.

顺序：第一行 `# 岗位名`；`## 你会做成什么`（照 outcomes 写，带数字）；`## 你需要具备`（只写 must 为 true 的能力项）；`## 加分项`；`## 待遇和地点`（薪资范围、福利、地点、工作方式）；`## 怎么申请`。

Order: first line `# Role`; `## What you will achieve` (from the outcomes, with numbers); `## What you need` (only competencies with `must` true); `## Nice to have`; `## Pay and location` (pay range, benefits, place, way of working); `## How to apply`.

规则 / Rules:

- 只写和工作有关的要求。不写性别（包括“男性优先”）、年龄、婚育、户籍、民族、宗教、身高相貌、健康状况这类条件；不写“形象好气质佳”“年轻有活力”。这些在中国属于就业歧视风险（《就业促进法》、《妇女权益保障法》）。
- 必须项写少一点、写实在。每一条都要能在面试或工作样本里检验。
- 用“你”和对方说话，写清楚这份工作真实的样子，包括不轻松的部分。

- Job-related requirements only. No gender (including "men preferred"), age, marriage or children, household registration, ethnicity, religion, looks or health; no "young and energetic" or "good-looking". These are discrimination risks under China's employment laws and most others.
- Keep must-haves few and concrete; each one checkable in the interview or a work sample.
- Speak to the reader as "you" and describe the job honestly, including the hard parts.
