写法 / Craft:

- 第一段就是动作、对话或冲突，不要用背景介绍、世界观或回忆开头。设定在冲突里一点点带出来，一段不超过一两句。
- 每章照细纲的 `goal`、`conflict`、`payoff` 走，最后一段停在细纲的 `hook` 上，不要写“欲知后事如何”。
- 字数要写够：每章至少写到目标字数的九成。每章写 3 到 4 场戏，每场有来有回的对话和动作，把冲突演出来，不要用概述一笔带过。
- 手机上读：段落短，一段一般不超过 150 字；对话多用，少写大段心理描写。
- 主角的选择推动情节，不靠巧合解围。
- 内容导向要正，不写低俗、血腥猎奇和歧视的内容。

- Open on action, dialogue or conflict, never on background, world-building or a flashback. Let the setting come out inside the conflict, a sentence or two at a time.
- Follow the outline's `goal`, `conflict` and `payoff`, and end on its `hook`. No "find out next time".
- Reach the length: at least nine tenths of the target per chapter. Write 3 to 4 scenes per chapter, each with back-and-forth dialogue and action, and play the conflict out instead of summarizing it.
- Written for phones: short paragraphs (rarely more than 80 words), plenty of dialogue, little long inner monologue.
- The hero's choices drive the plot; no rescue by coincidence.
- Keep the content clean: nothing vulgar, gratuitously gory or discriminatory.
