# 创作说明：米粒的黄帽子

> 示例是虚构的。换成你自己的说明。

## 给谁读

- 4 岁的米粒和她的同龄人（3 到 6 岁），睡前由大人读给孩子听。
- 孩子还不认字，主要看图；文字要好念，念出来有节奏。

## 想讲的故事

一阵大风把米粒最喜欢的黄帽子吹走了。她一路追，帽子先被小老鼠当成船，又被青蛙当成伞，最后挂在树上，一只小蓝鸟正往里面衔树枝做窝。米粒想了很久，决定把帽子留给小鸟。第二年春天，黄帽子里探出三只小鸟。

## 想要的感觉

- 舍不得，又是自己愿意的。分享是米粒自己的决定，没有人要她这么做。
- 不讲道理，也不要大人出来告诉她该怎么做。

## 角色

- 米粒：4 岁女孩，黑色短发，齐刘海；黄帽子上别着一朵白色小雏菊；蓝白条纹上衣，红色雨靴。
- 豆豆：米粒的小狗，棕色，一只耳朵是白的，总跟在她脚边。
- 小老鼠、青蛙、小蓝鸟：都是第一次见到米粒。

## 一定要有

- 帽子上的白色小雏菊，最后一页的鸟窝里还看得到它。
- 一句可以反复念的话，孩子能跟着说。
- 最后一页不用字，让图来说。

## 不要有

- 危险动作：米粒不爬树，不独自走到深水边。
- 吓人的画面。

## 书的样子

- 32 页，正方形，8.5×8.5 英寸。
- 水彩，颜色柔和。
- 献词：献给真正的米粒。

# Brief: Millie's Yellow Hat

> This sample is fictional. Replace it with your own brief.

## Who it's for

- Four-year-old Millie and children her age (3 to 6), read aloud by an adult at bedtime.
- They can't read yet and mostly follow the pictures; the words should be easy to read aloud, with a rhythm.

## The story

A big wind blows away Millie's favourite yellow hat. She chases it: first a mouse uses it as a boat, then a frog as an umbrella, and at last it hangs in a tree, where a little blue bird is carrying twigs into it for a nest. Millie thinks for a long time and decides to let the bird keep the hat. Next spring, three baby birds peek out of the yellow hat.

## The feeling

- Hard to let go, and still her own choice. Sharing is Millie's decision; nobody tells her to.
- No lessons, and no grown-up telling her what to do.

## Characters

- Millie: a four-year-old girl with a short black bob and a fringe; a yellow hat with a small white daisy pinned to it; a blue and white striped top and red rain boots.
- Beans: Millie's little brown dog, one ear white, always at her heels.
- The mouse, the frog and the little blue bird: meeting Millie for the first time.

## Must have

- The white daisy on the hat, still visible in the nest on the last page.
- One line that comes back, which children can say along.
- A wordless last page; let the picture say it.

## Must not have

- Unsafe behaviour: Millie doesn't climb the tree or go near deep water alone.
- Scary pictures.

## The book

- 32 pages, square, 8.5 × 8.5 in.
- Watercolour, soft colours.
- Dedication: For the real Millie.
