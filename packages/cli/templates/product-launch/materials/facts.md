## 产品是什么 / What it is

- 店小算 App 的新功能：用手机拍一张纸质小票，金额、日期和商家自动记进账本，记错了可以改。 / A new feature of the ShopTally app: take a photo of a paper receipt, and the amount, date and vendor go into the books, ready to edit.
- 英文名 / English name: ShopTally Snap

## 给谁 / Who it is for

- 一个人或两三个人打理的小店：咖啡馆、书店、花店。店主自己记账，进货小票攒了一抽屉。 / Small shops run by one to three people: cafés, bookshops, florists. The owner keeps the books and has a drawer full of supplier receipts.

## 现在怎么做 / What they do today

- 月底把小票摊在桌上，一张张敲进表格。 / At month end, spread the receipts on a table and type them into a spreadsheet one by one.
- 请兼职会计每月来一次。 / Pay a part-time bookkeeper to come once a month.
- 用功能很全的财务软件，大部分功能用不上。 / Use full accounting software and ignore most of it.

## 只有它有的 / What only it has

- 小票在手机上识别，照片不上传服务器。 / Receipts are read on the phone, and the photos are never uploaded.
- 识别结果直接进店小算的账本，不用导出导入。 / The results go straight into the ShopTally books, with no export or import.
- 中文和英文的小票都能读。 / It reads receipts in Chinese and English.

## 卖点 / Selling points

- 拍一张小票，账就记好了。 / Snap a receipt and it's booked.
- 照片留在手机上。 / The photos stay on your phone.
- 记错了随时改，原图一直跟着这笔账。 / Fix any entry at any time; the original photo stays with it.
