# ShopTally launches Snap: snap a receipt and it's booked

ShopTally Snap is a new feature for small shop owners who keep their own books: receipts are read on the phone, and the photos are never uploaded.

SHANGHAI, Dec. 1, 2026 — ShopTally, which makes a bookkeeping app for small shops, today launched ShopTally Snap. Owners take a photo of a paper receipt with their phone, and the amount, date and vendor go into the ShopTally books, ready to edit. It is available today in version 3.2 of the ShopTally app, on iOS and Android.

Small shops run by one to three people, such as cafés, bookshops and florists, often keep their own books, and their owners collect a drawer full of supplier receipts. At month end they spread the receipts on a table and type them into a spreadsheet one by one, pay a part-time bookkeeper to come once a month, or use full accounting software and ignore most of it.

ShopTally Snap moves that work to the moment the delivery comes. The owner snaps the receipt, and the results go straight into the ShopTally books, with no export or import. Receipts are read on the phone, and the photos are never uploaded, so supplier prices and vendors stay with the shop. It reads receipts in Chinese and English. Any entry can be fixed at any time, and the original photo stays with it.

From October to November 2026, 41 small shops used ShopTally Snap for three weeks in a beta. "Month end used to cost me an evening of typing up receipts. Now I snap them when the delivery comes," said Shen He, owner of Half Acre Books, who had free Pro during the beta.

"People who run a shop have enough to think about every day, and the books shouldn't take another evening," said Ji Bai, founder of ShopTally. "We wanted receipts booked when the delivery comes, with the photos staying on the owner's own phone."

The first 30 receipts each month are free; beyond 30, ShopTally Pro costs 18 yuan a month. ShopTally Snap needs version 3.2 or later of the ShopTally app, on iOS or Android.
