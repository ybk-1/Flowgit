# FAQ: ShopTally Snap

## Public FAQ

### What is ShopTally Snap?

A new feature of the ShopTally app: take a photo of a paper receipt, and the amount, date and vendor go into the books, ready to edit.

### Who is it for?

Small shops run by one to three people, such as cafés, bookshops and florists, where the owner keeps the books and gets a lot of supplier receipts.

### How do I get it?

Update the ShopTally app to version 3.2 or later, on iOS or Android.

### What does it cost?

The first 30 receipts each month are free; beyond 30, ShopTally Pro costs 18 yuan a month. The free tier has no end date.

### Are my receipt photos uploaded?

No. Receipts are read on the phone, and the photos are never uploaded.

### Can it read receipts in English?

Yes. It reads receipts in Chinese and English.

### What if it reads a receipt wrong?

Fix the entry at any time. The original photo stays with it, so you can check against it.

### Can it file my taxes?

No. Snap puts your receipts into the ShopTally books; filing taxes stays the way you do it now.
